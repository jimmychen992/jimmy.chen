Research

Experimental and computational mechanics for polymers, composites and lightweight structures, from material behaviour to engineering application.

01

Impact and dynamic mechanics

High-rate deformation, impact damage, ballistic response and energy absorption under short-duration loading.

Split-Hopkinson pressure bar · ballistic testing · damage and failure Related publication →
02

Architected structures

Lightweight, bio-inspired and horseshoe-microstructure lattices with programmable nonlinear mechanical response.

Lattice design · optimisation · additive manufacturing Related publication →
03

Polymers and composites

Rate-, temperature- and environment-dependent behaviour of biodegradable polymers (PLA, PCL, fibre composites).

Functional polymers · degradation · chemo-mechanics Related publication →
04

Measurement and modelling

Full-field experiments and physics-based models used together to explain and predict material response.

Digital image correlation · constitutive models · finite elements Related publication →
05

Constitutive modelling of glassy polymers

Experimental observation and finite-element modelling (elasto-viscoplastic framework) for glassy polymers that treats shear yielding and craze yielding as distinct deformation mechanisms.

Finite-strain plasticity · shear yielding · craze yielding · amorphous PLA Related publication →
06

Thermoreversible supramolecular polyurethane

Experimental characterisation and viscoelastic modelling of a healable supramolecular polyurethane across a wide range of strain rates and temperatures, demonstrating repeatable response over thermal cycles and prediction of high-rate behaviour from quasi-static data.

Self-healing polyurethane · viscoelasticity · time-temperature superposition · high strain rate Related publication →
