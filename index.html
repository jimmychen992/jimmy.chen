Mechanical Engineering, Materials and Education

Assistant Professor in Mechanical Engineering at NMITE*, Hereford, UK.

* NMITE: The New Model Institute for Technology and Engineering was incorporated in 2013 and registered as an English higher education provider in 2020. Its development has been supported by government funding, local partnerships and industry engagement; in 2026, NMITE was one of 24 institutions selected for a share of £80 million in UK government investment to expand student places and facilities supporting defence-sector skills. Based in Hereford, NMITE offers accelerated programmes in Mechanical Engineering, Integrated Engineering and Autonomous Robotics, alongside construction-focused provision.

I am an academic and professional engineer working across experimental mechanics, advanced materials science, structural design and engineering education. My research investigates how polymers, composites and architected structures deform, fail and absorb energy under demanding loading and environmental conditions.

I combine mechanical testing, digital image correlation, thermo-mechanical characterisation, constitutive modelling and finite-element simulation. I am also committed to practice-based, inclusive engineering education grounded in experiential learning, incorporating student-paced activities, interactive large-group sessions, 'learning by doing,' co-created activities, and Universal Design for Learning.
