Biography

Dr Jimmy H. Chen is an academic and professional engineer specialising in advanced materials science, solid mechanics and engineering education. His research connects experimental mechanics, constitutive modelling and structural design to develop lightweight, energy-absorbing and bio-inspired systems for demanding environments.

As a Chartered Engineer, Chartered Scientist and Chartered Physicist, he combines research practice with inclusive, experiential teaching. His work spans polymers, composites, architected lattices, impact engineering, advanced manufacturing and chemo-mechanical characterisation, alongside curriculum leadership and industry collaboration.

Research interests

  • Experimental mechanics and full-field measurement
  • Impact, high-rate deformation and material failure
  • Polymers, composites and biodegradable materials
  • Architected, lightweight and bio-inspired structures
  • Constitutive modelling and finite-element simulation
  • Advanced manufacturing and structural optimisation
  • Chemo-mechanical and thermo-mechanical behaviour
  • Practice-based and inclusive engineering education
